# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Report it privately on GitHub:
**Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository. Include what you
found, how to reproduce it, and the version or commit.

We reply within 7 days and tell you when a fix is released. We credit you in
the release notes unless you ask us not to.

## Supported versions

MasterRL has no stable release yet. Fixes go to the `main` branch.

## How MasterRL handles your data (by design)

- It runs on your own computer and your own GPUs. Nothing is uploaded
  automatically.
- Crash reports stay on your computer. You choose whether to attach one,
  redacted, to a GitHub issue.
- Secrets are kept in your operating system's keychain.
